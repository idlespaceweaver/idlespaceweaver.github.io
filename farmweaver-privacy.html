Privacy Policy - Farmweaver

Effective date: 17 October 2026

Farmweaver ("the game", package name com.timothywelch.farmweaver) is made by Timothy Welch ("I", "me"). This page explains what data the game handles. The short version: the game collects no personal data and I never receive any of your data.

Data collection

The game does not collect, store or send any personal information to me. There is no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. The game only uses the internet for the two things below: buying the Supporter Pack, and cloud saves if you choose to use them.

The Supporter Pack

The game has one optional purchase, the Supporter Pack. It is bought through Google Play or the App Store, who handle the payment. I never see your name, card or account details. The store only tells the game that the pack is owned, so it can unlock it.

Game saves

Your farms are saved on your own device. If you make a backup file, it is made on your device and you choose where to keep or share it. No copy is sent to me or to any server of mine.

Cloud saves

If you are signed in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iPhone), the game keeps a copy of each of your worlds there, so they come back on a new phone. These copies are stored in your own Google or Apple account. I cannot see them. The two are separate: a world saved through Google does not appear on an iPhone, and the other way round.

Sharing

If you choose to share a picture or a backup file, the game hands it to your phone's own share menu. Where it goes from there is your choice. The game does not see or keep a record of who you share with.

Deleting your data

To delete a world, press and hold it on the title screen and choose "Delete it". This deletes its cloud copy too. Uninstalling the game deletes everything on the device. Any backup files you made are yours to delete wherever you saved them.

To delete cloud copies yourself: on Android, open Google Play Games, go to your profile, then Settings, then Delete Play Games account and data, and choose Farmweaver. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage, and choose Farmweaver.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data from any player.

Changes to this policy

If a future update changes how the game handles data, this page will be updated and the effective date changed before that update is released.
