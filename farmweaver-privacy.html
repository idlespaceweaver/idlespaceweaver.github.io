Privacy Policy - Farmweaver

Effective date: 11 October 2026

Farmweaver ("the game", package name com.timothywelch.farmweaver) is made by Timothy Welch ("I", "me"). This page explains what data the game handles. The short version: the game collects no personal data and I never receive any of your data.

Data collection

The game does not collect, store or send any personal information to me. There is no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. The game does not use the internet.

Game saves

Your farms are saved on your own device. If you make a backup file, it is made on your device and you choose where to keep or share it. No copy is sent to me or to any server of mine.

Sharing

If you choose to share a picture or a backup file, the game hands it to your phone's own share menu. Where it goes from there is your choice. The game does not see or keep a record of who you share with.

Deleting your data

All the game's data is on your device. To delete it, press and hold a farm on the title screen and choose "Delete it", or uninstall the game. Any backup files you made are yours to delete wherever you saved them.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data from any player.

Changes to this policy

If a future update changes how the game handles data, for example by adding in-app purchases, this page will be updated and the effective date changed before that update is released.
